| 10 | Ytar residual distributions; six delta panels |
| 11 | Ytar residual versus y sieve; colors distinguish delta slices |

On pages 1–6, colors distinguish x-sieve columns. Graph markers show histogram means and vertical error bars show RMS widths, **not errors on the mean**. A centered, flat trend indicates smaller label-relative bias; large widths or structure call for inspection of individual holes. The source macros retain empty-bin graph points and histogram-range truncation; use the supplemental distribution reports to inspect empty bins and tails.

Legacy delta legends display nominal bin midpoints: the first says −15.5% because the metadata slice is [−25,−6), although the retained native event gate restricts its actual events to (−15,−6). There is no new delta cut.

## Compatibility and source audit

- Source commit: `d4dc427f8c260b868a7d4844e2707236a421601b`. Original files, adapted copies, exact diffs and SHA256 hashes are preserved in [reproduce/nps_legacy/](reproduce/nps_legacy/).
- The HMS 9×9 grid and 2.54/1.524 cm pitches become the already-authorized SHMS 11×11 grid and 2.5/1.64 cm pitches. These changes affect plotting bins only; input labels and geometric targets are unchanged.
- File paths and metadata are explicit arguments. The legacy delimiter-based metadata reader is replaced with the existing checked reader: three foils and exactly six intervals from seven edges. This avoids a spurious next-record boundary or missing foil.
- HMS angle captions become SHMS angle captions. Histograms, graph calculations, ranges, display shifts, page layouts, disabled canvases and plotting statistics otherwise follow the original bodies.
- In the original ytar macro, page 10 and the delta overlay use a +0.035 cm display shift; the unshown 2D ytar histogram uses −0.035 cm. Page 11 has no shift. These are inherited display conventions, not fitted target or matrix corrections.
- Every histogram bin and entry count, including underflow/overflow, was compared with the previously generated legacy outputs. They are identical after the documented SHMS adaptation. Thus the original residual-macro calculations were already present; the missing source provenance and poor navigation were real presentation problems.
- `SCRIPTS/plotOptics.C` and `plotOpticsSimple.C` are replay-tree analyses with calorimeter/Cherenkov cuts and, for plotOptics, invariant-mass branches. The minimal TFit handoff does not contain those measurements. They cannot be executed faithfully on this package; no synthetic replay/PID branches or new event cuts were introduced.
- Both matrices and fitted populations remain unchanged. No full HCANA replay was performed.
